Mishmar Protocol

movement & combat slice · no economy
Click a battery — draw an energy packet
Move — the packet is your grip; dim drones bend in
Press and hold — commit the squad, stop gathering
Drag — carry them, energy burning the whole way
Release — set them down to patrol that sector
Space — pause · R — restart · M — mute

Timer and budget are the same variable. The ring on the pointer is the packet burning down, and the number beside it is what you'd get if you set the squad down right now — it drops every time another drone joins, and it keeps dropping while you carry them. Distance costs power. Gather three and drop them close and they burn white; gather twelve and haul them across the field and they arrive faint. Run the packet dry mid-carry and they fall dim wherever you happen to be standing.
